# Security and privacy policy

This is an academic repository; there are no services or secrets. The relevant risks are privacy and copyright.

- Never commit credentials, student registration numbers (RA), lecture slides (`assets/courses/` is git-ignored) or
  unpublished third-party material.
- `assets/` is git-ignored except its README: drafts (student RAs), the professor's brief and slides, and the benchmark
  thesis stay on local machines; the Ashby chart PDFs stay local too. The team's own screenshots of the Granta software are in `docs/figures/granta/`. Do not force-add them.
- To report an exposure, contact the repository owner privately through GitHub, give the commit and file path, and do
  not reproduce the sensitive content in an issue.
